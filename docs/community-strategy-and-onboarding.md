# Community Strategy and Member Onboarding (TEMPLATE)

> Template with a **fictional sample community** ("Example Learners Club", an imaginary study-and-skills group). No real community, employer, or client is described, and no real numbers are used.

## 1. Purpose and audience

| Question | Sample answer (fictional) |
|---|---|
| Why does the community exist? | A friendly place for beginners to learn skills together |
| Who is it for? | Students and early-career learners |
| What do members get? | Peer support, study sessions, event invitations |
| What do we ask of members? | Respect the [code of conduct](code-of-conduct-and-moderation.md); share and help |

## 2. Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Community manager | Strategy, content calendar, metrics, escalation |
| Moderators (volunteers) | Welcome members, enforce guidelines, flag issues |
| Event hosts / speakers | Run sessions; follow the [event playbook](../events/event-playbook.md) |

## 3. Onboarding journey

| Step | When | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Join | Day 0 | Automatic welcome message with 3 links (rules, intro thread, events) | Community manager |
| 2. Introduce | Day 0 to 2 | Member posts in the intro thread; a moderator replies personally | Moderator |
| 3. First action | Day 1 to 7 | Invite to one easy action: reply to a thread or join an upcoming session | Moderator |
| 4. Check-in | Day 7 | Short message: "Anything unclear? What would you like here?" | Moderator |
| 5. Deepen | Day 14 to 30 | Invite to volunteer, co-host, or lead a topic if interested | Community manager |

### Welcome message (sample text)

> Welcome to Example Learners Club! Three things to start: (1) read our short guidelines, (2) introduce yourself in the intro thread, (3) see what is coming up on the events page. Not sure where to begin? Reply here and a human will help.

## 4. Member journey stages (for thinking, not scoring)

Lurker → first post → regular contributor → helper → co-organiser. Each step should have a low-friction invitation, not pressure.

## 5. Content pillars

| Pillar | Example formats | Cadence (sample) |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | Weekly question thread, short how-to posts | Weekly |
| Connect | Intro threads, member spotlights (with permission) | Fortnightly |
| Events | Study sessions, talks, workshops | Monthly |
| Recognise | Thank-yous, contributor shout-outs | Monthly |

The scheduled version is in [`templates/content-calendar-template.csv`](../templates/content-calendar-template.csv) and a filled fictional example in [`data/content_calendar_sample.csv`](../data/content_calendar_sample.csv).

## 6. Feedback loops

- Quarterly short member survey (3 to 5 questions)
- Monthly moderator sync (30 minutes): top questions, issues, ideas
- Review [engagement metrics](engagement-metrics.md) each month and decide one thing to try
