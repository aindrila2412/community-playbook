# Code of Conduct and Moderation Guidelines (TEMPLATE)

> Template for a **fictional sample community**. Adapt, and have it reviewed by your community and, where relevant, legal or HR advisers. This is not legal advice.

## 1. Our standards

We want a welcoming, respectful space.

### Please

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy: do not share others' personal information.
- Keep posts relevant and credit sources.

### Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Threats or intimidation
- Unwanted sexual attention
- Spam, scams, or undisclosed promotion
- Sharing private information without consent

## 2. Scope

These standards apply in all community spaces (channels, events, direct messages arising from the community) and when representing the community elsewhere.

## 3. Reporting

- Use the Report button, or message a moderator or the contact address (placeholder: `conduct@example.invalid`).
- Reports are handled in confidence; share only with those who need to know.
- Reporters will be told the outcome in general terms where appropriate.

## 4. Moderation ladder

| Level | Situation (examples) | Typical response | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor off-topic or tone issue | Friendly public or private nudge | Any moderator |
| 2 | Repeated issue or clear rule break | Private written warning, remove content | Moderator, informed lead |
| 3 | Serious harm, harassment, threats | Temporary suspension; document | Community manager |
| 4 | Severe or repeat serious harm, illegal content | Removal from community; escalate to platform/authorities as required | Community manager with senior input |

Use judgement: context matters, and the ladder is a guide, not a script.

## 5. Moderator principles

1. Act promptly and proportionately.
2. Be consistent; record decisions in the moderation log ([template](../templates/moderation-log-template.csv)).
3. Moderate in private where possible; thank people for good-faith reports.
4. Never moderate a case you are personally involved in; hand it to another moderator.
5. Look after yourself: take breaks and escalate when something is distressing.

## 6. Sample responses

### Level 1 nudge

> Hi, thanks for joining the discussion. That last reply came across a bit sharply. Could you rephrase so it focuses on the idea? Thanks!

### Level 2 warning

> Hi, we removed your post because it breaks our guideline on undisclosed promotion. Please read the guidelines here. Further posts of this type may lead to a suspension. Reply if you would like to talk it through.

## 7. Appeals

A member can ask for a decision to be reviewed by a different moderator or the community manager within a stated period (for example 14 days).
